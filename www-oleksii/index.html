What I do

Not restyling screens. Making products coherent.

I come in when a product, or a group of products, has grown complex, inconsistent, or unclear, and I make it coherent again. Not as a designer hired to restyle a screen, but as the person who investigates the real problems, sets the direction, builds the process and the team, and owns it through delivery.

Discovery & system analysis

I investigate how the product actually behaves, find the gap between what is assumed and what is real, and reframe the problem before anyone designs a solution. Requirements analysis, system investigation, root-cause and gap analysis.

Redesign & consolidation strategy

I turn fragmented products and inconsistent interfaces into one structured system, with a clear direction, a roadmap, and a prioritized backlog the team can build from.

Process & delivery ownership

I set up the workflow, requirements, and the activities for designers, engineers, and QA, then stay to own the product as it grows. I run the program; I don't hand it off.

How I work

Analytical, data-driven, hands-on

I ground product decisions in user research, behavioral analytics, and system investigation rather than opinion, and I build the analytics and process a team needs to find problems and prioritize them by real impact.

In practice this means decomposing a vague request into structured work, tracing a flow to its root issue, documenting actual versus expected behavior, and turning findings into action items engineers can ship from.
