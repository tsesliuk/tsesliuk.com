Case study2017 to 2019Cambium Networks · via GlobalLogicProduct Designer, Research & Presale

Network management software you can use in the field.

Cambium builds wireless networking hardware managed through software that had grown complicated and inconsistent. I evaluated the system, interviewed experts, and proposed the design direction that made it usable, outdoors included.

Client
Cambium Networks (Networking)
Years
2017 to 2019
My role
Product Designer, Research & Presale
Products
Network management software, UI kit, products catalog
The problem

Complicated software, expensive support, lost contracts.

Business problems
  • Complicated, inconsistent software made support expensive and lost contracts
  • The company did not know how to present its new interfaces
User problems
  • Users were confused by inconsistent components
  • No clear system status feedback
  • The dark design was hard to use outdoors during installations
The solution

A light theme for the field, and a catalog that sells the UI.

After a system evaluation against UX heuristics and expert interviews, I proposed a design system with a light theme for outdoor use, reduced-interaction controls, and clearer system feedback.

A products catalog presents each hardware item alongside its UI, giving sales a way to show the interfaces, not just describe them.
