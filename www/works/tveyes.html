Context

A 25-year-old product carrying outdated UX patterns into a real-time AI era.

TVEyes delivers near real-time transcripts, metadata, audio and video from nearly 3,000 channels across 27 countries. Users are PR analysts, communications professionals, and crisis teams who watch their organisations' presence across global media every minute.

The legacy product had grown organically over two decades. Power was high, usability had not kept up. Users couldn't filter by sentiment, share clips fluently, or work from the field. There was no design system, and every release widened the inconsistency.

AgileEngine engaged me to lead a full design program. Not a redesign sprint, but a discovery-driven rebuild with a design system that would outlive the project, and a hand-off rigorous enough to support React and MUI development end to end.

The work expanded into a second iteration, the Twosion AI Release, adding sentiment analysis, smart alerts, accessibility mode and AI-powered entities on top of the foundation laid in phase one.

Process

A seven-phase program, not a hand-off-and-leave engagement.

Each phase produced a documented artifact and a defensible decision before the next began. This is the lifecycle the team and I followed for the initial release.

Discovery did the heavy lifting. Systems evaluation, competitor analysis, user and stakeholder interviews, a Customer Journey Map, and information architecture. Nothing moved to prototyping until the problems were named and agreed with the client.

Prototyping then validated direction in Maze before a single high-fidelity screen was drawn. Decisions were defensible because they traced back to a discovery artifact, not a preference.

High fidelity was built entirely on the design system, so hand-off was a matter of linking libraries, not redrawing screens. The dev team received prototypes, a hand-off report, and demo videos from one index.

The program did not end at release. Twosion AI extended the same foundation, and documentation kept the product team moving without me in the room.

Discovery

Four user problems we wrote on the wall, and refused to ship without solving.

Discovery interviews, system audits and CJM workshops surfaced the same patterns repeatedly. We narrowed the program to four problems and tied every later design decision to one of them.

User Problems
  • Old design with outdated UX patterns
  • User can't schedule alerts by sentiment
  • User doesn't know which mentions are negative
  • User can't share events or reports
Solutions Shipped
  • Design system based on Material UI
  • Smart alerts: spike, gap, and sentiment triggers
  • Sentiment analysis surfaced on every event
  • Sharing across clips, events and reports
Primary persona "Larry", a Public Relations Analyst at a small agency. Every later decision was checked against his day.
We framed discovery with the Double Diamond, so every problem had a clear path from challenge to outcome. Interviews, a system audit and CJM workshops fed the same four problems.
Design System

A Figma library built on Material UI. Atomic, accessible, owned by the product.

Every button, icon, and input field is an atomic element. Branding, visual hierarchy, and WCAG 2 Level AA contrast were built into the tokens, not patched at the page level.

Token-driven colour system with state variants: hover, focus, selected, disabled.
Buttons across size, colour, and state variants.
The component library at a glance: buttons, colour styles, inputs, datepickers and sliders, each documented and ready for the team to reuse.
Design system promo. The hand-off video that walked the engineering team through tokens, components, and how to connect the library to their Figma files.

Multi-platform

Desktop primary, mobile companion, email second screen.

Desktop carries the analyst's daily work: power search, reports, media centre. Mobile keeps users connected away from a desk. Email templates handle alert-driven re-entry into the product.

Authentication: reset password, accessibility mode, and Material controls. Designed once in the system, rendered consistently on web and mobile.
Clip viewer with synced transcript, scrubbing timeline, and trim controls. The surface analysts spend the most time on.

Twosion AI Release

The second iteration: AI-powered entities, sentiment, and smart alerts.

Once the foundation was stable in production, we ran a design spike on AI features. Discovery, prototyping and high fidelity in compressed cycles. Sentiment analysis, smart alerts and AI entities were added without breaking the design language.

"AI Entities", a user-friendly big-data grid with cards, filtering and AI features stitched into existing patterns from the design system.

Smart Alerts. Spike detection, gap detection and sentiment shift detection. An analyst is alerted to a developing story before they refresh a search.

Sentiment Analysis. The emotional tone of mentions per keyword. Day-by-day score, distribution between positive, neutral and negative, with peak markers for live coverage.

Accessibility Mode. High-contrast mode meeting WCAG 2 AA, validated with heatmap analysis so users find primary actions equally well in both modes.

Heatmap-Driven Iteration. Every release iteration validated through heatmap analysis. Data-driven decisions, enhanced usability, streamlined navigation.
