"It's not working." Usability testing found out why in a week.

JLL shipped an MVP of a property portal, and management who used the old system said it was not working, without saying what was wrong. I ran remote usability testing with end users and turned the complaint into a fix engineers shipped in one day.

Client
JLL (Real estate)
Years
2018
My role
UX Designer & Facilitator
Products
Property portal MVP (B2B real-estate management)
The problem

A shipped MVP and a complaint nobody could explain.

Business problems
  • Management who used the old system complained the new MVP was not working, without explaining the problem
User problems
  • Users were confused about how to find a property in the new portal
The solution

Two findings, one unified list, one day to ship.

Remote usability sessions with end users produced two findings. First, users completed four of five tasks largely because of large screens, while management worked from small laptops. Second, users searched for clients in the property list and for properties in the client list.

I proposed one unified list with a single search and filter, removing clients and properties as separate pages. The development team implemented the change in one day.
