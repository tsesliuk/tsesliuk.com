The platform

Metric Insights is a business intelligence platform. Teams monitor data, dashboards, and alerts across devices. It had to stay consistent and usable as it grew across web, native, and hybrid apps.

My role

I owned the UX end to end

Research and usability tests, interface design for web, mobile, and watch, and the detailed specifications the Scrum team built from. One design language across every platform, held together by a shared system.

Web dashboard

The core BI experience

Data, charts, and alerts in one place, designed for daily monitoring. The dashboard scales from a single metric to a full operations view.

Mobile

The platform follows users

Native and hybrid apps carry the same data to small screens. Layouts adapt, the design language stays the same.

Apple Watch

Metrics on the wrist

Key numbers and alerts at a glance, focused on what needs attention right now. The hardest constraint shaped the clearest design.

Themeable UI

One interface, many themes

A themeable system so the platform adapts to brand and context without rebuilding screens. Tokens drive color, type, and spacing.

Email digests

Data that comes to you

Scheduled digests and alerts delivered by email, designed to match the platform and read well in every client.

Large screens

Auto-rotating views for big screens in operations centers and lobbies, readable from across the room.
