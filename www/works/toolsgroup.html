One entry point for a whole family of supply chain products.

ToolsGroup, a supply chain and retail planning SaaS, had grown into a family of products with inconsistent UX. I led a full design program: discovery, design strategy, and the design team through Discovery, Design, and Hand-Off.

Client
ToolsGroup (Supply chain SaaS)
Years
2021 to 2023
My role
Design Program Lead, UX Management
Products
Desktop web app, design system
The problem

Many products, many logins, one frustrated planner.

Business problems
  • Many products with inconsistent UX and UI
  • Dated look and feel; drift across the product family
User problems
  • Users log into several different tools to complete daily tasks
  • Help and support are not available to the end user
  • Users do not learn about new functionality after software updates
The solution

A unified experience layer, an embedded Academy, and a design system.

Discovery research fed a design strategy that the team executed end to end. We delivered a unified experience layer as a single entry point across the product family, plus an embedded Academy with in-product tutorials so users learn new functionality where they work.

A design system (foundations, components, patterns) prevents the inconsistency from re-emerging as the products evolve.
